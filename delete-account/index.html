Delete your Hawula account

You can delete your account yourself, any time, from inside the app. It takes less than a minute.

Steps

  1. Open Hawula and go to Profile → Help & support → Delete my account.
  2. Make sure you are squared up. You need a zero balance in every group first, so that nobody's debt disappears by accident. If you still owe or are owed money, the app will tell you to settle up first.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

What happens straight away

  • Your login, name, email address, phone number and profile picture are removed.
  • Your notification details are deleted, and you are signed out on every phone.
  • The comments you wrote are deleted.
  • You leave all your groups. A group you ran passes to the member who has been there longest, and a group you were alone in is erased.
  • Your repeating bills stop.

What stays

Bills and repayments you shared with other people stay, so their balances remain correct. They show “Deleted user” and carry no personal details. Deleted details are also cleared from backups within 30 days.

Can't open the app?

Email hawula.app@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”, from the address you signed in with, and we will review it. If you still have an unsettled balance, we will tell you what needs to happen first.
