How Hawula works

A quick look at the basics.

Three simple steps

  1. Start a groupMake a group for a trip, your flat or the office lunch, and invite friends with a code.
  2. Add what you spendAdd an expense and choose who shares it. Everyone's balance updates on its own.
  3. Settle upSee who owes whom, pay in whatever way suits you, and record it here.

Common questions

How is a bill split?

Pick who shares it, then how: equally, by exact amounts, by percent, or by the items on a receipt. Hawula works out what each person owes the one who paid.

How do I settle up?

Open the group and tap Settle up. Hawula shows the fewest payments that square everyone up. Pay however suits you (cash, bank transfer, card, an online wallet…), then tap Mark as paid so the balances catch up.

Can I add bills without signal?

Yes. Bills and payments added without signal are kept on your phone and sent by themselves as soon as you're back online.

How do repeating bills work?

When adding a bill, set Repeat to every week, month or year, and optionally an end date. Hawula adds it again when it's due. Stop it any time from the group's Repeating bills.

What does the app lock do?

Under Profile → Security, Hawula can ask for your phone's own PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face before it opens. Hawula never sees or stores them.

How do I delete my account?

Settle up in every group first, then go to Help & support → Delete my account. Your past bills stay for the others, under "Deleted user". Step-by-step guide
