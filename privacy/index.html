Hawula Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Hawula helps friends, families, and small groups split bills and keep track of who owes whom. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. If you use the guest option, we create an anonymous account without requiring a name or email address.
  • Groups and expenses. We store the groups you create or join, member display names, bills, amounts, notes, comments, repayments, balances, and activity history that you enter or generate through the app.
  • Notification information. If you enable push notifications, we use a code that identifies your phone so we can deliver relevant alerts.
  • On-device preferences. Your language, theme, text size, and app-lock choices are stored only on your device.
  • Support information. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. The in-app “Contact support” action includes your app version, Android version and phone model to help troubleshoot problems; it does not automatically attach your bills or balances.

To work without signal, the app keeps a copy of your groups and any unsent bills on your phone. It is removed when you sign out.

How we use your information

We use this information to provide sign-in, maintain your groups, calculate and display shared expenses and balances, send notifications you have enabled, answer support requests, and keep the service functioning.

We do not sell your personal information, display advertisements in Hawula, or use your information for advertising.

Who can see or receive your information

Other group members. People in a group can see its members’ display names, bills, amounts, who paid, who owes what, comments, repayments, and related group activity. Only add information you are comfortable sharing with that group.

Service providers. We use service providers to support features such as sign-in, data storage, and push notifications. They process information to provide services to Hawula. We do not share your information with advertisers.

How long we keep information

We keep account and group information while it is needed to operate Hawula. When an account is deleted, we remove its sign-in details, notification details, name, email address, and profile picture as described below. Shared bills and repayment records remain so other members’ group balances and history stay accurate; the deleted member is shown as “Deleted user” with no personal details attached. Comments you wrote are deleted.

Deleted account details are also cleared from our backups within 30 days. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years, and technical records only as long as needed to resolve problems and keep the service secure, or to meet legal obligations.

Your choices and privacy rights

  • Access or correct information. You can contact us at the email below to request access to, or correction of, information associated with your account. We reply within 30 days.
  • Delete your account. In Hawula, go to Profile → Help & support → Delete my account. You need a zero balance in each group first, so that shared debts are not accidentally lost. Deletion happens immediately: your login, name, email, phone number, profile picture, notification details and the comments you wrote are all removed. The bills and repayments you shared with others stay, so their balances remain correct, but they show “Deleted user” with no personal details attached.
  • How to delete your account. Step-by-step instructions are on the account deletion page.
  • Need deletion help? If you cannot complete in-app deletion because of an outstanding balance or another issue, email hawula.app@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request” so we can review your request.
  • Leave a group. You can leave a group through the app once your balance in that group is zero. When you leave, your name stays on the group's past bills and repayments so the group's records stay accurate. To remove your name everywhere, delete your account.
  • Manage notifications. Turn them off in Profile → Notifications or through your phone’s settings.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information. Contact us to ask about your options.

Security

Hawula sends information over encrypted connections. You can also protect access to the app using your phone’s PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face authentication under Profile → Security. Hawula does not receive or store your device PIN, pattern, or fingerprint.

No online service can guarantee complete security. If you believe your account or information is at risk, please contact us.

Where information is processed

Service providers supporting Hawula may process or store information in countries outside the country where you live. Privacy protections may differ by location.

Children’s privacy

Hawula is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, contact us so we can review it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Hawula evolves. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app where appropriate.

Contact us

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or account deletion assistance, email hawula.app@gmail.com.
