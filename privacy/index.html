Hawula Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Hawula helps friends, families, and small groups split bills and keep track of who owes whom. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Hawula, operated by Rasheed Rafi, Sri Lanka (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from Google. Accounts created earlier with the former guest option are anonymous and have no name or email address.
  • Payment details you choose to add. If you add ways to be paid back (such as bank account details or a payment link), we store them and show them to the people you currently share a group with, so they can pay you. You can remove them at any time, and they are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Groups and expenses. We store the groups you create or join, member display names, bills, amounts, notes, comments, repayments, balances, and activity history that you enter or generate through the app, along with your default currency. Each group also has an invite link code so people can join it.
  • Notification information. If you enable push notifications, we use a device notification token, along with your device platform (Android or iOS), to deliver relevant alerts.
  • On-device information. Your language, theme, text size, and app-lock choices are stored only on your device. To work without signal, the app also keeps a copy of your groups and any bills not yet sent on your phone; this copy is removed when you sign out.
  • Support information. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. The in-app “Contact support” action includes your app version, Android version, and phone model to help troubleshoot problems; it does not attach your bills or balances.

How we use your information

We use this information to provide sign-in, maintain your groups, calculate and display shared expenses and balances, send notifications you have enabled, answer support requests, and keep the service functioning and secure.

We process your information because you ask us to provide the service (to run your account and groups), because you have chosen to turn on a feature such as notifications or app lock, and because it is necessary for our legitimate interests in keeping Hawula working, secure, and supported. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You choose what to give us. Your name (or a display name) and the bills you enter are needed to use groups; if you do not provide them, or you refuse notifications, some features will not work, but you can still use the rest of the app.

We do not sell your personal information, display advertisements in Hawula, or use your information for advertising.

Who can see or receive your information

Other group members. People in a group can see its members’ display names, bills, amounts, who paid, who owes what, comments, repayments, and related group activity. Only add information you are comfortable sharing with that group.

Service providers. We use service providers to support features such as sign-in, data storage, and push notifications. They process information only to provide services to Hawula. We do not share your information with advertisers.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or Hawula.

How long we keep information

We keep account and group information while your account is active, because it is needed to operate Hawula.

When an account is deleted, its sign-in details, notification token, payment details, name, email address, and profile picture are removed straight away, along with every comment the person wrote, and from our backups within 30 days. Shared bills and repayment records remain so other members’ group balances and history stay accurate; the deleted member is shown as “Deleted user.” A group the person was alone in is erased, with everything in it.

Bill names and notes are typed by group members and stay with the shared bills. If one of them still mentions you after your account is deleted, email us and we will remove your name from it.

Support emails are kept for up to 2 years after the conversation ends, then deleted.

Your choices and privacy rights

  • Access or correct information. Email us at the address below to ask for a copy of, or a correction to, the information linked to your account. We respond within one month. If a request is complex, we may extend this by up to two more months and will tell you why before the first month ends. If we refuse a request, we will tell you why and that you can appeal to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.
  • Withdraw consent or object. You can withdraw consent, or object to how we use your information, by emailing us or by deleting your account. We do not make decisions about you using automated processing alone.
  • Delete your account. In Hawula, go to Profile → Help & support → Delete my account. Deletion requires a zero balance in each group, so that shared debts are not accidentally lost. After deletion, your account identifiers and profile details are removed, while shared transaction history remains with your identity replaced by “Deleted user.” Step-by-step instructions: How to delete your Hawula account.
  • Need deletion help? If you cannot complete in-app deletion, email hawula.app@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request” from the address you signed in with, and we will help.
  • Leave a group. You can leave a group through the app once your balance in that group is zero. When you leave, your name stays on the group’s past bills and repayments so the group’s records stay accurate. To remove your name everywhere, delete your account.
  • Manage notifications. Turn them off in Profile → Notifications or through your phone’s settings.

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information. Contact us to ask about your options. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can contact us first, and you also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka.

Security

Hawula sends information over encrypted connections. You can also protect access to the app using your phone’s PIN, pattern, fingerprint, or face authentication under Profile → Security. Hawula does not receive or store your device PIN, pattern, or fingerprint.

No online service can guarantee complete security. If you believe your account or information is at risk, please contact us. If a data breach affects your information, we will notify the Data Protection Authority and the people affected, as the law requires.

Where information is processed

Our main service providers are Supabase (data storage and sign-in), Google (Google sign-in), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). They may process or store information in countries outside Sri Lanka and outside the country where you live. Privacy protections may differ by location. We choose providers that protect information under contract terms, and we send only the information needed for each service. Sending your information to these providers is necessary to provide Hawula to you; by creating an account you agree to this, and you can withdraw that agreement at any time by deleting your account.

Children’s privacy

Hawula is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child under 16 has provided information to us, contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Hawula evolves. We will change the “Last updated” date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.

Contact us

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, or account deletion help, email hawula.app@gmail.com.
