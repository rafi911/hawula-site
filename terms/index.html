What Hawula is

Hawula is a tool for friends, families, and small groups to record shared bills and keep track of who owes whom. Hawula does not hold, move, or process money. Repayments you record in the app are notes that a payment was made somewhere else (cash, bank transfer, or another service). They are not payments themselves.

Balances and debts shown in Hawula are informal records kept by group members. They are not legally binding contracts, and we cannot guarantee that what members enter is accurate.
